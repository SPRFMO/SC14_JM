Research findings, proposals and agreed decisions are distinguished throughout this guide.

Suggest an addition or correction

Help make the guide useful to fisheries managers while preserving the qualifications in the science.

For a new finding, explain the management question, what the research found, why it matters, and what remains uncertain. Identify a public paper or meeting report and the relevant page or paragraph.

For a meeting update, give the date, what was discussed, and whether the wording is a proposal, a draft or an agreed conclusion. Include conditions or disagreement that affect interpretation.

This is a public working synthesis of the sources cited. Additions should be supported by public research or meeting records suitable for this audience. Discuss proposed updates through the working group’s usual channels.
